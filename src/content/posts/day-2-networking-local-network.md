So I learned about everything that makes up a network. And it all starts at your router, which
connects your local network to your ISP.

And then that router connects to your switch. Then that switch usually plugs into a patch panel,
which connects to your building's wired infrastructure.

And that's how servers, workstations, wireless access points, and any other devices connect to your
local network.
