Can I make a modded Minecraft server in under 5 minutes? Wish me luck.

So I'm remoted into my server, just making a Minecraft server for me and my friends. This feels way
more official than it actually is.

It's actually pretty simple:

1. Download the pack you want. I'm going with Crazy Craft for this one.
2. Extract the files.
3. Wait for it to start.

Okay, I don't mean to jinx myself, but I think I did this first try.

This took 3 hours. I'm mildly upset.

And we're finally in. Server is running for the most part. A couple hiccups on the way, but it's
whatever.
