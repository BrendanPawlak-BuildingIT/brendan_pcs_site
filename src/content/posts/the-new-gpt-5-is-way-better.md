So I've been messing around with the new GPT-5 that came out, and it's a major improvement.

The biggest thing I noticed is that it sounds way more natural, and the speed is pretty insane too.
Think about all the times AI got something wrong — this new version should fix that. I gave it a
very specific task and it nailed it.

But that's not the coolest part. It seems to be using something called mixture of experts, where the
AI can route your prompt to a specialized submodel that can best respond to it. And that's where it
gets its PhD level of intelligence.

Also, apparently it's really good at coding.
