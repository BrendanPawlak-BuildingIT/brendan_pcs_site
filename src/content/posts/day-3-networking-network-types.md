Day three of learning networking every single day until I get my Network Plus.

Today I learned that not all network types are the same. They're actually broken down into different
categories based on how big they are.

- **Personal area network** — essentially just your phone connecting to your AirPods.
- **Local area network** — all the devices in your house, like your phone and gaming PC.
- **Wireless LAN** — the same, but just for the wireless portion of your network.
- **Virtual LAN** — a way to split a physical network into multiple separated networks, like one for
  computers, servers, and entertainment devices.
- **Wide area network** — connects multiple LANs together.
- **Metropolitan area network** — all the interconnected LANs in a city.
- **Campus area network** — your college's or school district's network.
