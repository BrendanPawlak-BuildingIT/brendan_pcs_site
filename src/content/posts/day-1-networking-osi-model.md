This is day one of learning networking every day or I lose my job.

So the first thing I'm running into is this thing called the OSI model. These are the seven layers
that show how data travels from one point to another in a network.

It starts with just the signal, like the wired or wireless.

Now that signal becomes data and gets labeled with the MAC address of the device it's going to.

This is where routers and switches read your IP address and tell your data where to go next.

Now that data gets cut into pieces and put back together on the other end. And if it's missing
anything, it'll just ask for it again.

This is just keeping track of your connection, so if it drops, it doesn't have to go all the way
back to the beginning.

Then it gets decrypted or encrypted and compressed, and basically turns into something readable.

And then we're done. Your browser gets it, and this is what you actually see.
